Clear expectations before booking

Booking policies.

We review every request and confirm the scope, location, timing and payment terms before reserving an appointment.

Deposits

For jobs priced at $300 or less, the booking policy is a $50 deposit. For jobs priced over $300, the deposit is 20% of the quoted service total. The deposit is applied to the service total. The exact amount and payment method are confirmed before an appointment is reserved.

Rescheduling and cancellations

One complimentary reschedule may be requested with at least 24 hours’ notice, subject to availability. Contact Enhance My Ride as early as possible if your plans change. Any deposit handling follows the terms disclosed when booking and applicable law.

Service scope and pricing

Published prices are starting amounts. Vehicle size, condition, requested services and the agreed scope can affect the final price. We provide and confirm a quote before work starts. Specialty work is custom quoted.

Mobile appointments

We serve suitable residential and workplace locations across the nine Bay Area counties. Exact address access and any travel fee are confirmed before booking. Enhance My Ride brings its own water and power; customers generally do not need to provide either.

Requesting service

Submitting the online form is a request, not a confirmed appointment. Enhance My Ride will follow up using the contact information provided. A booking is confirmed only after the service scope, location, schedule, price and deposit terms are agreed.
