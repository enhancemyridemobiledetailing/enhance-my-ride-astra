Booking policies ↗
Do you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. Enhance My Ride is a mobile detailing business. We confirm the exact service location and access details when reviewing your request.

Do I need to provide water or power?

We bring our own water and power, so customers generally do not need to provide either.

Which Bay Area locations do you serve?

We serve suitable locations across Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, Napa, San Francisco, San Mateo, Santa Clara, Solano and Sonoma counties. We confirm address availability and any travel fee before booking.

Are the displayed service prices final?

Prices shown are starting points. Vehicle size, condition and requested scope can affect the final quote. Specialty services are custom quoted, and additions are agreed before work begins.

Can I send photos with my request?

Yes. The secure detail request form accepts up to five vehicle photos so we can review the condition and requested service.

How do deposits and rescheduling work?

See our booking policies for current deposit and rescheduling details. We confirm payment terms before an appointment is reserved.
