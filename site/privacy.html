Privacy policy.

How Enhance My Ride handles information submitted through the website and the optional Google review display.

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Information you provide

The detail request form collects your name, email address, phone number, service city, vehicle details, selected service, preferred date and time, condition notes and any vehicle photos you choose to upload. These details are used to review and respond to your service request.

Booking records and photos

Booking request details and uploaded photos are stored in the private booking system and retained for up to 90 days, then automatically deleted from its database and private photo storage. Email notifications sent to Enhance My Ride’s mailbox are outside this website cleanup period.

Google Maps reviews and photos

If the live Google Places feed is enabled, the site requests current review and business-photo data from Google Maps when the page is viewed. The site does not save that review or photo content in its booking database or object storage. Reviewer profile images load directly from Google; business-photo media is delivered through a no-store proxy. Google Maps content is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and Places API policies.

Security and contact

The booking form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent spam, and the site uses Cloudflare infrastructure to receive requests. Do not include payment card numbers or unrelated sensitive information in the request form. For a privacy request, contact info@enhancemyride.com.
