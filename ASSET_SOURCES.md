# Asset sources and licensing record

## Enhance My Ride business assets

The logo, favicon, Honda CR-V mobile-rig photo, and the 20-image original gallery set were copied into this isolated project from the existing Enhance My Ride website repository. The 13-photo Mercedes collection and 16-photo Lexus IS 250 collection were copied from the owner-supplied `EnhanceMyRide_Mercedes_WEBP_images` and `EnhanceMyRide_Lexus_Gallery_WEBP` folders in Downloads. These are treated as business-owned assets supplied by the owner. Source copies were left in place and no existing project assets were overwritten.

The four hero videos (`foam.mp4`, `rinse.mp4`, `finish.mp4`, and `hero-phone.mp4`) and their poster frames were recovered from the Astra Labs concept site at the following URLs:

- https://enhancemyride.astralabs.store/assets/film/foam.mp4
- https://enhancemyride.astralabs.store/assets/film/rinse.mp4
- https://enhancemyride.astralabs.store/assets/film/finish.mp4
- https://enhancemyride.astralabs.store/assets/film/hero-phone.mp4

The business owner confirms that the detailing footage in that mockup is original Enhance My Ride content sourced from its social accounts. Visual inspection shows detailing footage and no Astra Labs logo, promotional overlay, or agency graphic embedded in the frames. Vehicle and equipment manufacturers' marks appear incidentally in some footage. The original social-account masters were not available locally for a byte-for-byte comparison, so the ownership conclusion relies on the owner's confirmation; the video files' provenance is not independently established here.

## Third-party fonts

- **Barlow Condensed** and **Manrope** are loaded from Google Fonts. Google Fonts metadata lists both families as SIL Open Font License (OFL) fonts. The official repository records Barlow Condensed as OFL and provides its license; Manrope metadata also lists OFL.
  - https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/METADATA.pb
  - https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/OFL.txt
  - https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/METADATA.pb

No icon library, JavaScript framework, stock photography, or other third-party visual asset is included. Arrows and separators use standard text characters. Google Reviews and social-network links are business integrations rather than bundled creative assets.

## Implementation

Site HTML, CSS, and JavaScript were independently written for this repository. The reference site was used only to understand the requested general mood and content themes; its source code and proprietary site graphics, scripts, styles, logos, and promotional copy were not reused. The layout and panel composition were independently changed for this version.
